Fame

Suddenly as you enter the room you are surronded by more money then you have ever seen. Millions of dollars and priceless artifacts, a security guard in the room tells you that there are men outside waiting to help you gather and transport the money and artifacts to your new mansion.

You live the life of one of the richest most luxurious billionaires to ever exist. But sometimes you wonder, what if this wasn't the right path, was the money really worth it? What else lied behind those other doors?.
